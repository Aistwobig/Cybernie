Bernie's Tavern

Cybernie

A cozy multiplayer pixel-art tavern for the web. Pick a hero, walk in, grab a seat and hang out with your friends, in text, voice, video and on the big screen upstairs.

Mclaren is sharing

  1. Step inside.

    One room, up to twenty players, all moving in real time. Walk with WASD, the arrow keys, or the joystick on your phone.

  2. Bernie's pouring.

    Order from the bartender with your coins. Every drink does something: Goblin Cider makes you quick, Tavern Ale makes the room sway.

  3. Pull up a chair.

    Stools, side chairs, the seats behind the big table: every character sits the right way on every one of them, by the crackling fire.

  4. Read the board. Climb the stairs.

    The notice board has news from the tavern. The stairs lead up to somewhere new.

  5. Share your screen.

    The meeting room upstairs has a projector. Put your screen on it, sound and all, for everyone in the room, or open it full screen.

Six heroes

Who's walking in tonight?

Each one has their own walk, idle and sitting animations. Hover or tap a card to send them on their way.

Proximity voice chat

Talk like you're in the room.

Voices get quieter as you walk away, and drop out when you're out of earshot, just like a real tavern. Turn your camera on and your video floats above your head.

  • Straight from browser to browser: nothing is recorded.
  • Pick your mic, camera and speaker in Settings.
  • The music dips while people talk.
