# Demo video

**Watch:** [youtu.be/jBdyOs0AQtU](https://youtu.be/jBdyOs0AQtU)
**Length:** 12:38
**Recorded on:** a computer browser, with the app in Device Preview's phone
frame (portrait for the menus, landscape in the tavern), with friends joining
from their own devices.

The video is on YouTube because it is too big for the repo (GitHub blocks files
over 100 MB).

## What it shows

- **0:00** The landing page: what Cybernie is, the heroes, voice, friends,
  blackjack, the lucky wheel and the Android app.
- **1:28** The splash screen and signing in with Google.
- **1:38** Home: the character preview, music and sound settings, and night
  mode.
- **2:07** Friends: the friends list, friend requests and sent requests.
- **2:30** Private messages with emotes.
- **2:52** Profile: changing the name, the profile QR code and choosing a hero.
- **3:15** Choosing a room and walking into Bernie's Tavern.
- **4:16** Voice chat and cameras above the players' heads.
- **4:52** Ordering drinks from Bernie, and the tasks list.
- **5:26** Blackjack against Bernie.
- **6:54** Upstairs in the meeting room: everyone gathered, cameras on, sharing
  a screen on the projector.
- **9:09** More of the meeting room: screen sharing, emotes and chat.
- **10:34** Back downstairs at the bar: the room chat, private messages beside
  the room, the message notification (even during blackjack) and players'
  profiles.

## Privacy

Only friends who agreed to be in it appear. The Google account picker (which
shows email addresses) was cut out of the recording.
